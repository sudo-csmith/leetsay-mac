Third-party code inside Qt
==========================

Qt GUI and Qt Widgets, with their macOS (Cocoa) platform and image plugins.

Qt-third-party-full.txt in this folder. The full list is also published by the
Qt Project at

    https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/licenses-used-in-qt.html

(a third-party website that we do not run or control). For the modules
leetsay uses, these are:
